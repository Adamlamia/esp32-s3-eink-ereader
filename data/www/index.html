How to add a book
  1. Join the Wi-Fi network EReader-Setup (password read1234).
  2. Open http://ereader.local (or http://192.168.4.1).
  3. Drag a file into the box above, or click browse.
  4. On the device, hold the button to open the menu, then choose Library and pick your book.

Supported: plain-text .txt files saved as UTF-8 (books, notes, articles, scripts).
Not supported: PDF, EPUB, MOBI, DOCX — convert them to .txt first (e.g. export as TXT from Calibre).
